Growing more than a garden in Anii!

A small farming game with a bigger idea: making agricultural learning feel like part of the adventure.

Kirby BilbaoEarly draft / Development details to come

A note on this draft. This story introduces the project using public information and confirmed contributions. Specific development decisions, challenges, and outcomes are still to be added with Kirby.

What if tending a garden could also make learning about agriculture feel natural? That question sits at the heart of Anii! Grow to Know, an educational farming simulator created by student developers at Ukiyo Studios.

The idea

Players plant, water, and harvest Filipino Bahay Kubo crops, answer agriculture-themed quizzes, and decorate a garden as they progress. The game brings together hands-on play and agricultural literacy, giving players a reason to keep returning to their plots.

Building the experience

Built in Unity for an educational game jam, Anii combines farming, quiz challenges, progression, and customization in a low-poly 3D world. Kirby contributed across programming and game systems, game and learning design, art and interface, and team and release leadership.

The project brings different disciplines together: game systems need to support the play loop, the interface needs to make information approachable, and the game's visual world needs to feel inviting.

What comes next

This case study is a starting point. Details about the team's specific design decisions, technical challenges, and lessons learned will be added as the story develops.
