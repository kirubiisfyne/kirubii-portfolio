Making document formatting a game.

Inside KonTEXTo: an educational game about print jobs, careful editing, and the people behind the paperwork.

Kirby BilbaoEarly draft / Development details to come

A note on this draft. This story introduces the project using public information and Kirby's résumé. Specific development decisions, challenges, and outcomes are still to be added with Kirby.

Formatting a document might not sound like the beginning of a game. KonTEXTo takes that familiar task and turns it into an educational, narrative-driven editing simulator.

The idea

Players complete print jobs, interact with quirky characters, and work toward the perfect score. Along the way, document formatting rules become things to put into practice rather than simply read about.

Building the experience

KonTEXTo is an educational RPG capstone project built in Unity 6. As lead programmer and technical artist, Kirby developed gameplay mechanics and custom C# systems, and integrated game-ready 3D assets from Blender.

Combining a narrative setting with an educational goal gives the project room to make a precise, everyday skill more interactive.

What comes next

This case study is a starting point. More about the implementation, development challenges, and lessons learned will be added as the story develops.
